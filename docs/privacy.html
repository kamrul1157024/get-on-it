Get On It: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Get On It is a goal and habit app for Android. This page says what the app does with your data. The web version of this page is https://kamrul1157024.github.io/get-on-it/privacy.

In short

  • Your goals, progress, and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app has no account, no ads, and no analytics.
  • The app does not sell or share your data.
  • The app contains no AI. You paste plans from your own AI chat.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The plans you import, your progress, your reminder settings, and the nudge settings.
  • Files that you link, such as a PDF of a book. The app stores only where the file is. The file stays where it is.

Android backup

Android can back up the app's database (your goals, progress, and settings) to your own Google account. Android does this, not the app. It works only when backup is on in your phone's settings. Google keeps this backup under its own terms. You can turn it off in Android settings.

Usage access (optional)

If you allow usage access, the app reads which apps are open on your phone, and for how long. The app uses this only to:

  • nudge you when you spend too long in an app that you chose, and
  • show the "Time lost" card.

This data stays on your phone. The app never sends it anywhere. You can turn usage access off at any time in Android settings.

Display over other apps (optional)

If you choose the "Push me hard" nudge level and allow "Display over other apps", the app shows a stop screen on top of an app that passed its time limit. The app does not read what is on your screen.

Notifications and alarms

The app shows reminders and alarms for your goals. It plans them on your phone.

Internet

The app uses the internet only to:

  • play or open YouTube videos that are in your plans,
  • open web links that are in your plans,
  • open an AI chat website when you tap "Copy and open", and
  • in the version from GitHub (not the Google Play version), check GitHub for a new version.

These services have their own privacy policies. The app sends them no data about you beyond what opening the page needs.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13.

Delete your data

  • Delete a goal in the app to delete it and its progress.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all of its data on the phone.
  • To delete an Android backup, use your Google account's backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes, with a new date at the top.
